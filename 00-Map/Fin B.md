## Ground rules

1. **Paper only.** `FINB_ALLOW_LIVE` must equal a specific magic string before
   any live path opens, and the gate must independently pass. Both, or neither.
2. **$500.** Not the broker's $100k paper default. The risk engine enforces our
   number, because a strategy that only works at $100k is not our strategy.
3. **Free.** No paid data, no subscriptions, until something is demonstrably
   profitable out-of-sample.
4. **A negative result is a result.** "Nothing cleared the gate this cycle" is
   the expected outcome most cycles, and it is worth more than a promoted
   coin-flip.
